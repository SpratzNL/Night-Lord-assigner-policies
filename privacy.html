Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Night Lord Assigner Bot ("the bot") processes when you interact with it, how that information is used, and the choices you have. The bot is operated for the Night Lord MapleStory community Discord server(s) ("the operator", "we").

In short: the bot reads your verification message and screenshot only to confirm you are a Night Lord and to set your roles and nickname. Screenshots are never stored. The only data kept is a short troubleshooting log created when a verification fails, and that log is deleted once you successfully verify.

1. Information We Process

The bot processes only the minimum data needed to perform verification:

  • Discord identifiers — your Discord user ID and username.
  • Verification messages — the text you post in the verification channel (server name, in-game name, and letter count).
  • Uploaded screenshots — the character-card image you attach. The image is read into memory, scanned with on-device text recognition (OCR), and then discarded. The image file itself is never saved.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify that your submitted character is a MapleStory Night Lord.
  • To confirm the in-game name you provided appears on the character card.
  • To assign Discord roles after successful verification.
  • To update your server nickname to match your verified in-game name.
  • To diagnose and fix verification errors when a verification attempt fails.

3. Data Retention

Verification data is temporary by design:

  • When a verification attempt fails, a text-only troubleshooting log may be stored. It may contain your Discord ID, username, the message you submitted, and the text extracted from your screenshot — but not the image itself.
  • These logs exist solely to identify why verification failed.
  • Once you successfully verify, all troubleshooting logs associated with your account are automatically deleted. The operator may also delete them sooner.
  • Roles and nicknames set by the bot are managed by Discord and persist until changed by you or a server administrator.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to any third party. Text recognition is performed locally by the operator; your screenshot is not uploaded to any external service. Data submitted through Discord is also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request that any troubleshooting log relating to your account be deleted, or ask what data (if any) is currently held about you. Because logs are automatically removed on successful verification, in most cases no data about you is retained. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's Privacy

Discord requires all users to be at least 13 years old (or older where local law sets a higher age). The bot is not intended for anyone who does not meet Discord's minimum age requirement, and we do not knowingly process data from such users.

7. Security

Any stored data is limited to verification troubleshooting and is accessible only to the bot operator. We take reasonable measures to protect it, though no system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the bot after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
