Terms of Service

This bot is operated for use within a single Discord server and is not publicly distributed.

Purpose

The Night Lord Assigner Bot automates MapleStory Night Lord verification using user-submitted screenshots and messages.

Usage Rules

  • Users must submit accurate and unaltered verification information
  • Attempting to bypass or manipulate the verification system is prohibited
  • Only direct screenshots of MapleStory are accepted

Data Handling

Temporary logs may be created when verification fails. These logs are deleted after successful verification.

Service Changes

The bot may be modified or removed at any time without notice.
