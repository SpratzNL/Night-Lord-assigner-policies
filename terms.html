1. Purpose

The bot automates MapleStory Night Lord verification. It reads a verification message and a user-submitted character screenshot, confirms the character is a Night Lord, and then assigns the appropriate Discord roles and updates your server nickname to your verified in-game name.

2. Eligibility

You must comply with the Discord Terms of Service and meet Discord's minimum age requirement (13, or higher where local law requires) to use the bot.

3. Acceptable Use

  • Do not attempt to bypass, manipulate, spoof, or abuse the verification system.
  • Do not use the bot to impersonate another player or submit a character that is not yours.

4. Roles & Nickname Changes

By using the bot you authorize it to assign Discord roles to your account and to change your server nickname to your verified in-game name upon successful verification.
